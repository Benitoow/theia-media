# Theia 5.2.0 — the phone scans your server, the interface calms down, and updating Theia restarts it

**The Android application reads your server's QR code itself, its player is the desktop's bar made small, the gold-era gradients are gone, search rests on the connect screen's field of marks, and updating Theia now restarts the player so the new interface is the one you see.**

Theia 5.2.0 is a **Product Release** (decision 196) that follows 5.1.1 the same evening the maintainer went through the whole interface again, screen by screen, on the 5.1.1 build. The native player shells (Rust, libmpv) are the ones already pinned: nothing native changed. What reaches the desktop travels in the signed interface pack `theia-ui-5.2.0.zip`; the six servers and the setups are rebuilt around the pinned shells; the Android application is a new APK, because its shell gained a permission (below).

---

## What arrives with 5.2.0

### 1. The phone reads the server's QR code (decision 240)

- **A button on the connect screen:** *Scan a QR code*. The camera opens under a frame, the code is decoded inside the application (jsQR 1.4.0, Apache-2.0, fetched only when a scan starts), and the address it carries fills the field and connects. A camera application used to open that address in a browser; nobody has to copy it into Theia any more. Another code (a Wi-Fi sign-in, a website) is refused in words; a refused permission, a phone with no camera and Back are each answered.
- **One permission, `CAMERA`,** declared as *not required* so a television or a tablet without a camera keeps the application. The shell grants the page's own origin the camera and nothing else, and Android's question is put at the moment of the scan, never at launch. Frames are decoded and dropped: nothing is recorded or sent.
- **The server's QR code no longer points at a VPN.** On a machine running Cloudflare WARP the primary address, and so the welcome page's QR code, was the VPN adapter (`172.16.0.2`) instead of the Wi-Fi. VPN adapters now rank after real ones, and adapters named like a LAN's before unknown ones.
- The welcome page of the server says to scan the code from the application (French and English).

### 2. The phone's player is the desktop's bar, made small (decision 240)

- One row of 18–20 px icons under the timeline, the 44 px touch targets kept; no volume (the device's keys) and no fullscreen (the shell's). The island of the desktop shows on a phone too, at the top under the status bar.
- The button that opens the audio, subtitle and quality sheet is named for everything it holds ("Audio, subtitles and quality"); the sheet is narrower, smaller and pure black, and rests on the bar instead of covering it.

### 3. The interface calms down (decision 241)

- **No decorative accent gradient.** The underline under the wordmark, the glow in the profile button, the Theia+ pill and *Activate* gradients, the glows of the web viewer's pages and the site headline's gradient are flat or gone. Black scrims over artwork stay.
- **Search rests on the connect screen's field of marks** instead of a video loop: about 850 kB less in the interface, and it answers the pointer the way the connect screen does. Under reduced motion it draws one frame and stops.
- **The thumbs on a title page are squares.** The count is a badge on the corner, drawn only once somebody has reacted: no more empty space beside the icon, and a reaction still cannot move the row.

### 4. Updating Theia restarts the player (decision 241)

When the *Update* button staged an interface pack, it now says the player is installing, saves your position and, after a moment, restarts the player so the new interface is the one on screen. Before, the server updated and the player went on running the old interface until somebody restarted it by hand. A pack that is already the one served restarts nothing; if the restart is refused (a film is on screen) the Restart button of the *Player interface* group is still there. **The first update to 5.2.0 is done by the 5.1.x interface, which does not do this**: use that Restart button once; from 5.2.0 on updates restart on their own.

### 5. Smaller fixes

- A season the metadata names only "Season 2" is drawn in the interface's language ("Saison 2").
- A statistics poster that cannot load falls back to the not-found art instead of a broken-image icon.
- The Discord button of the Theia+ gate and the settings segments reach 44 px.
- The player's activity beacon no longer needs `crypto.randomUUID`, so a page served from a plain address still works.
- The README, the site's three product pictures, the share card and the phone and television pictures are retaken for the 5.1 interface.

## Updating

- **The desktop player (Windows, macOS, Linux):** the interface pack updates from *Settings › Updates*; use *Restart to update* once (see 4). No new shell.
- **The server:** the usual update, from the player or from the setup.
- **Android:** install `theia-android-5.2.0.apk`. It is signed with the same key as 5.0 and 5.1 and updates them in place; the camera permission is asked only the first time you scan.

## What was and was not checked

- **Verified:** the Go suite (45 packages), the player's full render bench (French and English, at 390, 969 and 1920 px), the connect bench with a camera that films the server's own QR code as `internal/discovery` draws it, the phone bench at three shapes, the update-restart bench (three cases), the site tests, the contrast guard and the decision record. A debug build of the native player carrying this interface was run on the maintainer's PC against a throwaway server and looked at by him.
- **Pictures:** the new README and site pictures come from the current interface in Edge, and the phone and television ones from Android emulators; the player picture is the interface over a stand-in backdrop, not a native capture (see `docs/screenshots/README.md`).
- **Not verified:** Android's own camera question and the camera inside the real WebView on a physical phone; the restart-after-update with a real published pack (it is asserted against the shell's documented answers); macOS and Linux native windows.
