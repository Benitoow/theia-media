# Theia 4.3.1 — friend requests that arrive

4.3.1 rebuilds friend requests between households. They no longer depend on
either of you opening a port on your router, and a request now waits for your
friend even when their computer is off. The server, library, ordinary viewing
and everything in this release remain free.

## Downloads

all-in-one, server-only or player-only during installation. The setup looks for
a newer setup before it installs, and works offline with what it carries.

**The ARM64 setups (Windows and Ubuntu) follow in early November**, once their
machines can run the native checks. Servers installed alone update themselves
to 4.3.1 on all six platforms.

**Upgrading:** from 4.3.0, the update button in the player installs
everything. From 4.2.1 on Windows, **Settings → Server & updates → Update
Theia** opens this setup; from anything older, run the setup by hand. Your
library, profiles, friends and watch history stay in their data directory.

**Both of you need 4.3.1.** A friend still on 4.3.0 shows as "Request sent"
until they update. Friendships you already had carry over by themselves once
both servers run 4.3.1.

## Friends

- **No open port, no router address.** Households now meet through a small
  database run for Theia (Firebase). Your server signs in to it anonymously:
  there is no account, nothing to type and nothing to switch on.
- **A request waits for somebody offline.** Enter a code and you are told at
  once whose it is; your friend finds the request in their Friends the next
  time their server runs, and accepts or declines it.
- **What your friends watch reaches you within a second or two**, and the
  friends sheet keeps up on its own while it is open. A friend whose computer
  stopped shows as offline.
- **Codes are shorter**: `THEIA2-XXXXX-XXXXX`. An older `THEIA1-` code still
  works.
- If the friends service cannot be reached, the sheet says so instead of
  showing everybody as gone.

## Privacy

Theia now speaks to one more service, only for friends in other households.
Firebase receives, under an anonymous identity, the names and pictures of
profiles that have friends elsewhere, requests between them and what each
friend is watching right now - nothing about your library or your history.

## Limits

- Watch parties still travel through Theia's encrypted tunnel, so hosting or
  joining a room in another household still needs remote access, which the
  friends sheet offers to turn on.
- Mac apps are ad-hoc signed and not notarised; Windows binaries are unsigned.
  Ubuntu still needs `libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0` and `libayatana-appindicator3-1`.
