# Theia 4.2 — the island, fullscreen and everyday reliability

4.2 brings profile pictures and cached film artwork into the island, refines its
motion, and fixes concrete playback, room, upgrade and resume-history failures.
The server, library, ordinary viewing and everything in this release remain free.

Run the new setup to upgrade. Your library, profiles, friends and watch history
stay in their data directory. Update both the server and the player: a server-only
update does not replace the native player.

On macOS and Linux, make the setup executable with `chmod +x` and its filename.
Mac apps are ad-hoc signed and not notarised; Windows binaries are unsigned.
Ubuntu still needs `libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0` and `libayatana-appindicator3-1`.

## The island

- Room members show their profile pictures, including through the native player's
  local connection. Initials remain the fallback for a missing picture.
- Cached TMDB posters and backdrops give the room its film's identity. Member
  arrivals and departures retain the right face when somebody changes their name.
- The shape opens and closes smoothly, with staged content and stable text.
  Fast reversals, narrow windows, keyboard focus and reduced motion are handled.
- Play, pause and ten-second seeks are available in the expanded island.
  Your personal resync catches up only your player; resetting your offset leaves
  the shared room's position unchanged.

## Fullscreen and window controls

- F and Escape still work after focusing the timeline. Timeline arrows continue
  to seek once per press. A fast Escape after F exits fullscreen without stopping
  the film, even before the UI has received the window's new state.
- Double-clicking the title bar changes the window once. A restored maximised
  or fullscreen window immediately shows the correct controls and resize state.

## Rooms

- Revoking sharing, ending the room, leaving or revoking the friendship stops
  active transfers. Event streams recheck membership and friendship.
- Joining the same room again keeps the existing membership instead of revoking
  the newly returned token.
- When two members are loading, the first recovery no longer resumes the film
  while the other is still waiting. The original ten-second cap stays in force.
- Losing the host pauses a locally owned copy. A borrowed stream stops when its
  room is lost or left; voluntarily leaving with your own copy keeps it playing.

## Installation and watch history

- The setup checks every component required by its role. An already-updated
  server no longer hides an older player or launcher, and missing native bundle
  files are repaired.
- Native direct launch reads the installation's chosen data directory and finds
  its server outside the macOS app or Linux player bundle.
- A deleted or consolidated title returning HTTP 404/410 no longer blocks valid
  resume points behind it. Temporary failures remain queued for another attempt.

## Security

The server is rebuilt with Go1.26.9 and the corrected network library. This
addresses the reachable advisories found during the release scan, including
[the HTTP/2 encoder advisory](https://pkg.go.dev/vuln/GO-2026-6617).

## Limits

- The friend's original 4.0 fullscreen symptom and operating system were not
  available. These changes address reproduced control failures; they cannot
  identify that particular machine's failure.
- The host's shared film is not converted. Guests need its original bandwidth
  and a player able to decode it. Rooms still need direct connectivity through
  the configured encrypted tunnel; Theia uses no third-party relay.
- Platform package checks do not establish HDR output or sync across arbitrary
  displays, receivers and networks.
