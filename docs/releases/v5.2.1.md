# Theia 5.2.1 — your pass follows you, a failed pairing says why, Drive films are read ahead, and the greys are plain

**The Theia+ pass you activate stays with this device and is offered to every server, a pairing that fails now says why, a film read from Google Drive is read ahead so a 4K remux has less reason to pause, a phone is flat black with no glow, every panel is a plain grey, the web settings and the player's server pane now draw the same things, and the word THEIA enters like a title sequence.**

Theia 5.2.1 is a **Product Release** (decision 196) that follows 5.2.0 the day after the maintainer used it on his laptop (the all-in-one), his tower (a server) and his phone. The native player shells (Rust, libmpv) are the ones already pinned: nothing native changed. What reaches the desktop travels in the signed interface pack `theia-ui-5.2.1.zip`; the six servers and the setups are rebuilt around the pinned shells; the Android application is a new APK, because the phone's page is packaged in it.

---

## What arrives with 5.2.1

### 1. The Theia+ pass follows the device (decision 242)

The pass used to be stored on each server, so activating it on one server left the next one asking for it again, and an update that reset a server's data lost it. The player now keeps the code you typed on this device and offers it again to any server that shows the gate: once per server per session, forgotten if a server says the code is invalid, kept if the network failed. Nothing is sent anywhere new: it is the same activation call, made for you.

### 2. A failed pairing says why (decisions 212 and 242)

Pairing two servers collapsed every failure into one sentence. It now says what happened, in the interface's own words, from three new codes: the other server **did not answer in time** (off, asleep or behind a firewall), **refused the connection** (nothing listens on that port) or **could not be reached** (no route, which is what a VPN can do). The server's log names the cause and the host. The web administration's pairing form says the same.

### 3. A film read from Google Drive is read ahead (decision 242)

Segments of a Drive film were fetched one after the other. After two contiguous reads Theia now fetches the next two segments while the player plays the current one, in memory only (at most 32 MiB, kept 45 seconds), never taking the last of the three Drive streams and cancelled as soon as the player leaves. Against a stand-in Drive with 200 ms of latency per segment, twelve segments took 1.75 s instead of 2.63 s. **This was not measured against the real Drive**; if your link is slower than the film's bitrate, nothing in Theia changes that.

### 4. A phone is flat black (decision 242)

On a phone the cards, the avatar tiles, the empty artwork, the banner and the glow behind the loading card were lifted greys and gradients; they are one flat colour now, or black.

### 5. Every grey is plain (decision 243)

The blue-grey is gone from the interface, the installer, the server's own page and the site: panels are `#333333`, a hovered row is `#404040`, hairlines `#4D4D4D`, and the text greys are neutral. A grey panel takes contrast from colours that are not grey, so the **error colour is lighter** (`#E58275`, 4.68:1 on a panel); the accent stays at 3.67:1 on a panel and is only used for large text and interface marks there.

### 6. The web settings and the player's server pane draw the same things (decisions 226 and 242)

- **The web administration** gains the image and preview caches (sizes and a clear button) and a *Presence and privacy* block in *Devices & access*: status, how long it lasts, when to count as away, share the title, share the last presence, return notices.
- **The player's server pane** gains *Connect a device* (the code, the address, the other addresses), *Google Drive* (accounts, folders, the search, disconnecting), *Access from outside* (the tunnel, the manual port and address, the devices and the one-time key of a new one), *This computer* and *Help request* (the report is handed over as a download); presence is a group of its own in its rail and search.
- **Signing in to Google stays in the administration**: Google returns to a one-shot listener on the server's own computer, and the shell can only open the server's `/settings` page in your browser. The player says where to go and opens it.
- No setting is left on only one surface.

### 7. THEIA enters like a title sequence (decision 244)

The word on the player's loading and connect screens, and above the pitch on the site's home page, sits in shadow, draws its rule, lets a light rise through the letters, a passage of gold turn them to bone, then breathes. It plays once and only while it can be seen; reduced motion shows the settled word.

## Updating

- **The desktop player (Windows, macOS, Linux):** the interface pack updates from *Settings › Updates* and the player restarts on its own (5.2.0 and later). No new shell.
- **The server:** the usual update, from the player or from the setup.
- **Android:** install `theia-android-5.2.1.apk` (version code 50201). It is signed with the same key as 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2 and updates them in place.

## What was and was not checked

- **Verified:** the Go suite and `go vet`, `npm audit` at high severity (nothing), the player's whole bench (French and English, 390, 969 and 1920 px, 131 render checks), the connect, presence, typography and target-size benches, two benches driving the new server groups against stand-in servers, the web's whole browser suite (147), the contrast guard (with the grey panel's pairs), the decision record, the settings-parity guard (no gap left), and the site's build, tests and large-text audit.
- **Not verified:** the read-ahead against the real Google Drive; the pairing failure on the maintainer's own tower (the new codes will name the cause the next time); anything on a physical phone or a television; the support report and the device `.conf` as downloads inside the packaged player; the new groups against a real Google account, router or WireGuard client; `check-presence-native.mjs` and `check:settings442`, the latter red since a top hairline was added to a metadata list in 5.2.0's tree (a design call left open).
